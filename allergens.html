Allergen information

Before ordering with an allergy or intolerance, speak to the owner or trained cafe team on 07423 772364 or email elitescaffe33@gmail.com. Please tell us the specific product and every selection, including White or Brown sandwich bread, muffin flavour, fillings, milk and syrup. We will check the available ingredient and supplier information before you pay, and confirm the relevant allergen information with you when you collect.

We use different ingredients and shared equipment in the cafe. Preparation areas and utensils are cleaned between tasks, but this does not guarantee freedom from cross-contact. If we cannot confirm the information needed for your allergy, we cannot promise the order will be suitable.

Some ingredients and supplier precautionary statements have not yet been fully verified for every finished dish and deal. An empty allergen list does not mean a food is allergen-free. Ingredient descriptions, supplier information and product photographs are not a complete allergen guide.

We keep the optional Order notes field so you can put relevant ingredients to avoid or preparation instructions in writing for our staff. Please do not include unnecessary medical diagnoses. For a serious allergy or where you need us to confirm an ingredient or handling request, speak to our team before payment. A written note by itself does not guarantee suitability or replace our ingredient check. Confirm the agreed details when collecting from 33 High Street, Leominster HR6 8LZ.
