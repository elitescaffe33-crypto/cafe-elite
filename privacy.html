Information we use

For collection orders, we collect your name, phone number, chosen products, quantities, collection time and payment/order status. An email address and order note are optional. We use this information to prepare and fulfil your order, contact you about it, handle refunds or disputes and meet relevant business and accounting obligations. Order information is intended for staff responsible for these tasks.

Why we use your information

We use the details necessary to take and fulfil your collection order to perform our contract with you. We keep the financial and transaction records required by applicable tax and accounting law to meet legal obligations. We also use proportionate order and technical information to secure the service, prevent duplicate or fraudulent payment attempts, and address customer queries or disputes, based on our legitimate interests in operating a reliable business and protecting customers. This is separate from promotional marketing.

Please do not put a medical diagnosis or unnecessary health information in the optional order note. Information revealing a food allergy can be sensitive health information, so contact our team directly before ordering to discuss the particular ingredients and preparation you need to avoid. Staff use relevant allergy instructions to assess and prepare the order; an online note alone does not establish suitability or guarantee prevention of cross-contact. We do not use allergy information for promotional marketing.

Optional order notes and your choice

If you choose to include a note, a separate, initially unchecked box asks for your explicit consent to use any food-allergy or health information in it solely to assess, prepare and hand over this order. This consent is separate from payment, general terms and marketing. You can remove the note and order normally without giving this consent. Changing the note requires you to confirm your choice again. Staff access is limited to authorised order handling; notes are not a guarantee that an allergy request can be safely accommodated.

For this optional health information, the intended lawful basis is consent under Article 6(1)(a) and explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) of UK GDPR, separate from the contract basis for ordinary order details. We record the order reference, server time, consent statement and version, purpose and your affirmative choice. We do not infer consent from an old order, a note alone or a phone call. Please contact us before including another person's health information so the appropriate consent arrangements can be established.

You can withdraw this consent by contacting elitescaffe33@gmail.com or 07423 772364. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal. Tell us the order reference if available; do not send unnecessary medical details. We review withdrawal and deletion requests manually, including any relevant copies in staff messages, and explain any genuine legal retention requirement. This website does not yet provide automatic deletion on withdrawal. Contact the team promptly if the order is still being prepared; removing permission to use the note may affect our ability to assess the requested preparation safely.

Payment and service providers

Online card payments are handled on Stripe's own checkout page, not our order form. Our website provides Stripe with an order reference, product names and selected choices, amounts and currency, and your email if supplied. Our website does not send your name, phone number, collection time or order note as Stripe Checkout metadata in the new ordering flow. Information entered on Stripe's payment page is processed by Stripe under its own arrangements.

Our website is hosted with Render and production orders are stored using Supabase. We use Stripe for card checkout. The cafe may also receive necessary order details through the email service selected for staff order notifications. These messages go to the cafe; entering an optional customer email address does not itself mean an automated customer confirmation email will be sent.

Processing outside the UK

Some service providers operate outside the UK. Our current website hosting is configured in a US region, while the production order database uses a European hosting region. Other provider operations and support access may involve additional locations. Contact us to ask about the processing locations and relevant international-transfer arrangements for your information.

Storage and retention

We keep order records and associated messages for order fulfilment, customer support, accounting and payment disputes. The relevant retention criteria are whether the order or a refund/complaint remains unresolved, whether information is necessary for a legal claim, and whether financial recordkeeping law requires us to keep the transaction. Financial records may need to be retained for longer than contact details or preparation notes. Our current server order system does not automatically delete old orders; we are formalising a separate record review and deletion process. You can contact us about unnecessary personal information or a deletion request, which we consider subject to legal retention obligations. The four-hour basket period below is not a server order-retention period.

Our approved policy for ordinary preparation notes is deletion 90 days after order completion, except where a documented, necessary legal hold applies. The technical deletion workflow is not yet implemented: this is not an automatic expiry promise. The review must cover notes, order-message copies, stored order JSON, staff notification emails and backup copies, without unnecessarily removing financial records that must be kept. Contact us to request review of a note or ask about progress.

Your basket, maps and fonts

The website keeps a basket of product references, quantities and selected options on your device, usable for approximately four hours after a basket change; expired browser entries are cleared when the site is next used, rather than by a background timer. A random checkout attempt reference may be held in session storage. The order form does not deliberately save your name, phone or notes in the browser basket.

Loading the map or opening directions connects to Google Maps. The website requests fonts from Google Fonts.

Marketing

We are not collecting email or SMS promotional opt-ins in this initial version of the new ordering form. Operational communications about an order are separate from promotional messages. Earlier recorded preferences are not treated as new marketing permission.

Your rights and questions

You may contact us to request access to your personal information, correction, deletion or restriction where applicable, or to raise a privacy concern. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you also have the right to object to that processing, subject to applicable law. You may have other rights depending on how we use the information, including data portability in relevant circumstances. Some financial or dispute records may need to be retained despite a request for deletion. We will review requests and respond according to applicable UK data protection law. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
