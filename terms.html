Collection ordering information

We want ordering to be straightforward. These terms explain our usual collection-order procedures; they do not restrict your statutory consumer rights.

Ordering and collection

Choose products, review your basket and any available deal discounts, confirm your contact and collection details, and select an available payment option. We may need to contact you about availability or timing before we can fulfil a request. Collection is from 33 High Street, Leominster HR6 8LZ, using London local time.

You can save or print this information page for your records. After a confirmed online payment, the site displays the order reference and collection details; please keep them. If you need a copy of your order information, contact our team. We do not currently promise an automatic customer email receipt.

We normally need around 15–20 minutes to prepare an order; the online selector allows approximately 20 minutes. Last-order times and opening hours are shown on the website. A final order may occasionally be collected shortly after closing, but this is subject to confirmation with the cafe, not a guaranteed extended opening time.

Prices and payments

Menu and checkout totals are the final consumer prices, with no additional service or collection fee. Available card and collection-payment methods are shown at checkout. Sandwich orders require a White or Brown bread choice, and muffins require a flavour choice; neither changes the displayed base price. Where selected, syrup is an extra £0.50 per drink; available milk choices have no surcharge. Online card payments are handled securely through Stripe. If payment appears uncertain, contact us before paying again.

Cancelling an order

If preparation has not started, contact us and we will cancel your order and refund any payment in full. Once preparation has begun, we review cancellation requests individually and may offer a full or partial discretionary refund depending on the circumstances. Nothing here limits your rights if goods are faulty, incorrectly supplied, or services do not meet legal requirements.

Unavailable items and mistakes

If an ordered item is unavailable, we will try to contact you and offer an alternative. We will not substitute a product without your agreement. If you do not accept an alternative, we will cancel the affected order and provide a full refund for it. If you selected an item by mistake, please contact us promptly; we will assess possible corrections or cancellation based on preparation progress.

Late or uncollected orders

If you do not arrive, we will try to contact you using the phone number provided. Food will only be held while safe to serve. Where we have prepared the correct order and you do not collect it, there is normally no automatic refund. We will consider exceptional circumstances individually, without affecting any statutory rights.

Allergies and questions

If you or anyone you are ordering for has a food allergy or intolerance, please check the available ingredient and allergen information and tell our team before placing or paying for the order. You can record relevant ingredients to avoid and preparation requests in the optional Order notes field so our team has your instructions in writing. For serious allergies or an uncertain ingredient, call 07423 772364 before paying. We use some shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee freedom from cross-contact. An order note alone is not an assurance that an allergy can be accommodated; the relevant information should also be confirmed when the order is collected.

A nonempty optional Order notes field requires a separate explicit-consent choice for any food-allergy or health information you include. You can remove the note and order without this consent. This is not part of accepting these terms and does not confirm allergy suitability. See how we use notes and how to withdraw consent.
